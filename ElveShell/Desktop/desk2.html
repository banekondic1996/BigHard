🖥️ Multi-Screen Settings

Each monitor gets its own desktop instance. Drag icons/widgets between them by moving to screen edges.
To open a desktop on another screen, launch the app again with:
nw . --screen=1
Icons moved to screen edges will transfer to the adjacent screen instance if running.
